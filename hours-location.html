Getting here

However you're traveling

BART & Muni

24th Street Mission BART is a four-minute walk. The 14, 49, and 12 all stop within two blocks on Mission Street.

On two wheels

Valencia has a protected bike lane the whole way. Racks directly outside, and a Bay Wheels dock on the 24th Street corner.

Driving & parking

Metered street parking on Valencia (two-hour limit) and a public garage on 21st. Weekend mornings fill up fast — transit is easier.

Coming in with a group?

The back room seats twelve and can be reserved outside peak hours. Send us a note and we'll hold it for you.
