Before you write

Quick answers

Do you take reservations?

Not for regular tables — it's first come, first served. The back room (seats twelve) can be reserved outside peak hours.

Can I bring my dog?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome at the sidewalk tables. Inside, it's service animals only — health rules, not our preference.

Do you cater?

Yes — filter coffee boxes, pastry platters, and standing office orders around the Mission. One day's notice is usually enough.
