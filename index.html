Why people come back

Good coffee, and somewhere to sit with it

No queue-and-go conveyor belt. Just carefully sourced beans, unhurried service, and a room that's easy to spend an hour in.

Roasted close to home

We buy small lots from Bay Area roasters and change the single origin every few weeks, so there's always something new on the pour-over bar.

Baked before you're awake

Croissants, morning buns, and a rotating galette come out of our oven from 6am. When they're gone, they're gone — that's the deal.

A seat that's yours

Fast Wi-Fi, plenty of outlets, and nobody hovering over your table. Stay for a meeting, a chapter, or the whole afternoon.

Our story

Ten years on the same corner

The Daily Grind opened in 2016 in a former hardware shop, with one second-hand espresso machine and a hand-painted sign.

Not much has changed since — we still pull every shot to order, still know most of our regulars by name, and still argue about the house blend. What has grown is the room: more seats, a proper pastry kitchen, and a team who genuinely likes being here.
